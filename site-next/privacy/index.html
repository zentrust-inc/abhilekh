Privacy notice

Privacy

Abhilekh is at the planning stage. The sample app at app.abhilekh.org holds only fictional SAMPLE · नमूना documents. Please do not upload real medical papers yet.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your mobile number, your email address, or your Google account ID (the sign-in method you choose). With Google we ask only for your email address and a basic ID, never your contacts, files or anything else.
  • A scrambled (hashed) copy of your PIN. We cannot read your PIN.
  • Short-lived codes (stored scrambled), and small cookies that keep you signed in on your device.
  • The sample records you confirm, and a log of the shares you make, which you can see and revoke.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not show advertising.
  • We do not share your records with anyone unless you create a share. Shares are time-limited and you can stop them at any time.
  • Abhilekh shows what the paper says. It gives no medical advice.

Services that help us run

The app runs on Microsoft Azure. Text-message codes are sent through Akash SMS. Google receives a sign-in request only if you choose Continue with Google.

This website and the film

abhilekh.org sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The film on the home page is hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap play; from then on, YouTube’s own privacy terms apply to that video. We use the privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) player.

Questions or deletion

Write to hello@zentrust.world and we will delete your account and what is stored with it.
